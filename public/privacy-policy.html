Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

PostureFix ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Camera data: PostureFix uses your device camera solely to analyze your posture in real time. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never stored, uploaded, or shared.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Supabase.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymized data about how you use the app (e.g. session duration, features used) to improve the product.
  • Purchase information: Subscription and purchase data is handled by RevenueCat and Google Play. We do not store payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the posture analysis features
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send important updates about the app (no marketing spam)
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

3. Camera Permission

PostureFix requires camera access to analyze your posture. All image processing happens locally on your device. We do not transmit, record, or store any camera footage or images.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — for subscription management (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play — for payment processing (Privacy Policy)

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

PostureFix is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
