Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PostureFix ("PostureFix", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use the PostureFix mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who is responsible for your data

PostureFix is operated by an individual developer (the "Controller" for the purposes of EU data protection law). For privacy questions, the contact details are at the bottom of this page.

2. Information we collect

We've designed PostureFix to minimize the data we collect. Specifically:

  • Camera input. The App uses your device's camera to detect body keypoints in real time using on-device machine-learning models (TensorFlow.js MoveNet). Camera frames, photos, and pose keypoints never leave your device. They are not uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers.
  • Anonymous account identifier. If cloud sync is enabled, the App creates an anonymous account with our backend provider (Supabase) and assigns you a random user ID. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information.
  • Progress statistics. When cloud sync is enabled, the App stores compact, non-identifying progress data tied to your anonymous ID — for example: posture level, exercise difficulty preference, current streak, total minutes practiced, number of completed days, and the date of your most recent session. We do not store individual scan results, photos, or keypoints in the cloud.
  • Subscription and purchase information. If you purchase a subscription or one-time unlock, the App receives a status flag (premium / free) from our subscription provider (RevenueCat). Apple processes the payment itself; we never see your card details, Apple ID, or billing address.
  • Device information. The App may read basic, non-identifying device information (such as platform and OS version) for compatibility and crash diagnostics.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • We do not collect health records or medical history.
  • We do not access your photo library, contacts, microphone, or location.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and do not show ads.
  • We do not perform cross-app or cross-website tracking, and we do not share data with data brokers.

4. Camera permission

PostureFix requests permission to use the camera so it can run posture analysis in real time. All processing happens on your device. The App does not record video, save photos to your camera roll, or transmit camera data over the network. You can revoke camera access at any time from your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the core features of the App (posture analysis, exercise programs, progress tracking).
  • To sync your progress between your devices when you sign in to the same anonymous account.
  • To validate and manage your subscription status.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

6. Service providers we share data with

The App relies on the following third-party processors. They are bound by their own privacy policies and our agreements with them:

  • Apple App Store / Apple In-App Purchase — processes payments and manages subscription billing. (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription receipts and entitlements. (Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase — hosts our anonymous-account backend and the progress-sync database. (Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube (Google LLC) — exercise demonstration videos are embedded from YouTube. When you view a video, YouTube may set cookies and collect data per its own policies. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain to limit this. (Privacy Policy)

7. International data transfers

Our service providers may host data in the United States and the European Union. Where applicable, transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to the United States are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Your rights (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under applicable data-protection law (GDPR / UK GDPR / FADP):

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • The right to restrict or object to processing.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your national data-protection authority.

You can exercise the right to deletion directly inside the App (Settings → Delete My Account & Data). For other rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Your rights (California, USA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA") and California Privacy Rights Act ("CPRA") give you the following rights:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • The right to delete personal information we have collected from you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Children's privacy

PostureFix is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Account deletion and data retention

You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time inside the App: open Settings and tap Delete My Account & Data. This permanently removes your progress-sync row from our database and signs you out. Any local data on your device is also cleared.

Subscription receipts retained by Apple and RevenueCat are governed by their respective retention policies; we cannot delete those records on your behalf.

The information and exercises shown are for general informational purposes only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program, particularly if you have an existing injury, condition, or are pregnant.

13. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect the limited information we store. Communication with our backend happens over HTTPS using TLS. Despite our best efforts, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
